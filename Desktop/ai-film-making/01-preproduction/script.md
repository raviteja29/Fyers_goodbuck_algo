# THE LAST MEMORY
## A Short Film Script

**Genre:** Sci-Fi Drama  
**Duration:** 3-5 minutes  
**Setting:** Near-future, 2030s  

---

## LOGLINE
In a world where memories can be digitally preserved, an elderly woman must decide whether to upload her consciousness before her final moment, choosing between digital immortality and natural death.

---

## TREATMENT

**THE LAST MEMORY** is a poignant exploration of humanity, technology, and what it means to truly live. The film follows ELENA (75), a retired artist facing terminal illness, as she grapples with the choice to use revolutionary "Memory Transfer" technology that promises digital immortality.

The story unfolds in three acts: Elena's initial resistance to the technology, a moment of revelation about what makes life meaningful, and her final decision that surprises both her family and the audience.

---

## SCRIPT

### FADE IN:

**INT. ELENA'S APARTMENT - LIVING ROOM - DAY**

*Sunlight streams through large windows onto canvases and art supplies scattered throughout a cozy living space. ELENA VASQUEZ (75), frail but spirited, sits in her wheelchair, painting with trembling hands.*

*Her daughter MARIA (45), professional and concerned, enters carrying a tablet.*

**MARIA**  
Mom, we need to talk about the procedure.

**ELENA**  
*(not looking up from her painting)*  
There's nothing to discuss, mija.

**MARIA**  
*(frustrated)*  
The doctors say you have weeks, maybe days. The Memory Transfer technology could give you forever.

**ELENA**  
*(setting down brush)*  
Forever as what? Data in a computer?

*Elena turns to face Maria, her eyes reflecting decades of wisdom.*

**ELENA (CONT'D)**  
Your father and I, we lived. We felt rain, we tasted your first birthday cake, we held each other through storms. Can your machine do that?

### CUT TO:

**INT. TECH FACILITY - CONSULTATION ROOM - DAY**

*Sterile, white room. DR. CHEN (40s), enthusiastic scientist, presents holographic displays to Elena and Maria.*

**DR. CHEN**  
Mrs. Vasquez, you'd retain all memories, personality, even creative abilities. You could paint forever, watch your great-grandchildren grow up...

**ELENA**  
But would I still be *me*? Or just an echo?

*Dr. Chen pauses, struggling with the philosophical weight of the question.*

**DR. CHEN**  
That's... that's something only you can decide.

### CUT TO:

**INT. ELENA'S APARTMENT - BEDROOM - NIGHT**

*Elena lies in bed, staring at a family photo on her nightstand. Maria sleeps in a chair beside her.*

*Elena reaches for her phone, struggling with her weakened grip. She opens a voice recording app.*

**ELENA**  
*(whispering)*  
Maria... if you find this... I want you to know that every moment of this life has been worth it. The pain, the joy, the uncertainty...

*She pauses, emotional.*

**ELENA (CONT'D)**  
I've been thinking about what makes us human. Is it our memories? Our thoughts? Or is it something more... something that can't be copied?

*Thunder rumbles outside. Elena looks toward the window.*

**ELENA (CONT'D)**  
I remember the first storm after you were born. You cried, and I held you, and in that moment, I wasn't thinking about preserving it. I was just... living it.

### CUT TO:

**INT. ELENA'S APARTMENT - LIVING ROOM - MORNING**

*Maria wakes to find Elena's wheelchair empty. Panic crosses her face.*

**MARIA**  
Mom?!

*She rushes around the apartment, finally finding Elena on the balcony, sitting in morning sunlight, working on her final painting.*

**ELENA**  
*(peaceful)*  
I've made my decision, mija.

**MARIA**  
*(tearful)*  
The procedure?

*Elena shakes her head gently, continuing to paint.*

**ELENA**  
This painting... when I'm gone, it will carry something no computer can capture. The weight of my hand, the choices I made with each brushstroke, the love I felt creating it.

*She looks at Maria with profound love.*

**ELENA (CONT'D)**  
That's my immortality. Not in a machine, but in you, in this art, in every life I've touched. That's enough.

*Maria embraces her mother as Elena adds the final stroke to her painting - a vibrant sunrise.*

**ELENA (CONT'D)**  
*(whispered)*  
Some things are too beautiful to last forever. That's what makes them beautiful.

### FADE OUT.

**THE END**
